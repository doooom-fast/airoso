## 1. Why Lake Murray on airoso's engine (v5-bare) feels bad — ranked by evidence

Measured on the two builds' own data on the same road: v4 = `claude/lm-v4`, v5 = `claude/lm-v5`. The line is v4's line:
v5-bare's swept line (`data/gate_centerline.json`) sits a **median 0.20 m / p95 0.49 / max 1.14 m** off v4's in plan
and **0.01 / 0.03 / 0.31 m** in height. (The 2.57 m median in ledger 0065 was the first, spherical-Earth conversion; the
installed v5-bare line was rebuilt on the ellipsoid.) So the difference is not where the road goes. It is what the road
is made of and what is beside it. Cross-sections: both blends (`loop.blend` at each branch) cut every ~12 m on v4's
line, 1,123 stations x both sides, top physical surface every 0.5 m out to 30 m.

1. **The road edge. v4 has a curb; v5 has a paved embankment.** 1 m past the lane edge v4 is `1KERB` (curb + walk) at
   **2,227 of 2,246** side-stations, with a **0.14 m** curb rise (median and p95). v5 is `1ROAD_shoulder` at **2,238 of
   2,246**: the same ROAD physics as the lane, **no rise (0.00 m)**, running on **4.75 m (median) / 11.6 m (p95)** past
   the lane edge before the grass. In v5 there is no edge to feel or see: the lane just keeps going as asphalt down the
   bank. Audit: v4 curb verts 100,295, v5 **0**. Source: v021 `ribbon.curb_sidewalk` (`scripts-v021/geometry/ribbon.py:112`,
   called at `blender/build_loop_blend.py:1242`: curb 0.14, face 0.08, walk 1.8, grade 1.2) vs airoso's
   `road_edge.profile: "shoulder"` → `ribbon.road_shoulder` (`scripts/geometry/ribbon.py:134`, `build_mesh.py:1544-1552`),
   which v5 had to use because the engine's sidewalk "stood up to 1.7 m over the falling yards" (0065).
2. **The ground beside the road. v4 holds it near road level; v5 drapes it to the hillside.** Ground height vs the lane
   edge, at 10 m out: v4 median **-0.18 m**, p5 -1.07, p95 +0.75; v5 median **-0.77 m**, p5 **-1.90**, p95 **+3.75**.
   Share of side-stations more than 1 m below the edge 10 m out: v4 **5.5 %**, v5 **29 %**. v4's assembler conforms the
   grid to the road over 20 m + a 16 m blend (`scripts-v021/geometry/ribbon.py:188`, `build_loop_blend.py:1281`),
   clamps it to ±22 m of the nearest road (`build_loop_blend.py:106`, `:1284`), carves 0.2 m under the ribbon (`:63`),
   keeps that graded surface within keep_surface_m when tiered (`:190`), and engine-july's prep pins a 3 m shelf + 6 m
   feather at deck-0.02 (`engine-july-snapshot/scripts/ac/build_kn5_loop.py:70-72`, `:996`). airoso drapes the verge onto
   the real 3DEP ground at the lane edge (`ROAD_LIFT_M 0.0001`, `GRASS_CLEARANCE_M 0.01`, `build_mesh.py:31-35`). That is
   the "hillside" in v5's 18 forced drops (`worst_gated_excursions` at 11.5-15 m lateral) and the visual mess: every
   road on a ridge or in a ditch.
   Same stretch, s 2,100, left side (v4 | v5): +7 m curb +0.24 | shoulder +0.00; +10 m grass -0.16 | shoulder -0.26;
   +13 m grass -0.22 | shoulder -1.00; +16 m grass -0.28 | shoulder -1.75; +18 m grass -0.72 | shoulder -2.37.
3. **Everything that is not the lane is missing.** v5-bare is the loop alone: `1ROAD_main` 8,980 verts covering only the
   13.5 km ring (x -1,983..2,605); v4's ROAD 26,944 verts reaches the Del Cerro sub-loop and the north network (x
   -3,047, y 3,187). v5-bare's chain (`claude/lm-v5` ship.json) has no `route_connectors / trim / weld_mouths / gap_mouths
   / pads / aprons / stubs / median / bridges / lamps / signs / bespoke`, and no `connectors.local.json` in its sync-in.
   v4's road kn5 holds 128 objects (mouth fills, 3 pads, an apron, 31 stub barriers, two bridge bodies, 21 lamp-post
   meshes, ~7,600 bespoke building blocks, signs); v5's 55 meshes are terrain tiles, the road, the shoulder and paint.
   Not a lane metric, but it is what "messy" looks like: a lone ribbon through 1.34 M grass vertices (v4: 441 k).
4. **The ribbon's cross-section at the four junction corners.** v021 sweeps 3 vertices a row (left, centre, right;
   `scripts-v021/geometry/ribbon.py:25`) and levels each fold pinch on the mesh (`tools/weld_mouths_blend.py`, ledger
   0050). airoso sweeps 2 a row (`scripts/geometry/ribbon.py:71`, `_cap_offset` `:62`) and v5 levels the inside edge in
   the ribbon (`ribbon.fold_level_deltas`, lm-v5 only), so the levelled edge twists the whole half-width. v5's worst
   kinks are there: s 2,270-2,276, 4-6 m left of centre, **5.29 %-pts** (College / Alvarado); v4's worst 12 are all at
   s 6,947-6,952 (4.8 %-pts). v5 bumps > 2.5 cm 0.45 /km against v4's 0, plus 2 vertex bumps (s 2,273, s 21).
5. **The profile's texture.** v021 smooths the measured Y once along the line (Gaussian σ 1.5 pts, 2 passes,
   `scripts-v021/geometry/smooth.py:60`, `build_loop_blend.py:1188`). v5's profile is `tools/profile_from_fixes.py`'s
   piecewise-linear interpolation between the authored fixes, then airoso's Laplacian (λ 0.45 x 4 on x, y, z,
   `build_mesh.py:503`) and corner rounding (`:530`) over it. Slope change per metre: v4 p95 **0.12**, p99 0.24 %-pts;
   v5 p95 **0.29**, p99 0.34 (2.4x). The grade sits on plateaus (4.00 %, 4.34 %, 5.00 %) and steps at each fix. Under the
   3 %-pt kink gate, but it is under the wheels the whole lap.
6. **Corner shape.** airoso re-rounds a line v021 already rounded: of 35 corners under 150 m radius on v4, v5's
   minimum radius is larger on **32** (median **+9 %**, max +24 %: s 1,291 133 → 166 m, s 9,808 70 → 87 m, s 1,269 107 →
   128 m); the four junction corners 8.8-11.7 → 9.8-12.6 m. Corners lose their shape a little; this makes them easier,
   not rougher.
7. **Not the cause:** crossfall (both flat: crown 0.000 m median in both; v5 had no `road_profile.cambers` and no
   `crossfall.json`), widths (median |dw| 0.00 m, p95 0.59), heights (p95 0.03 m), plan line (median 0.20 m).

### Where v021 and airoso share an ancestor, and where they split

- Both ribbons descend from prodrive-ac-builder's `scripts/geometry/ribbon.py` (0c558bb 2026-06-07, "1ROAD ribbon + GRASS
  mesh"); `curb_sidewalk` is the same function in both (2868fb8 2026-06-26, "curb+sidewalk road edge as one shared-seam
  profile"), `conform_terrain_to_road` / `upsample_grid` / `grass_terrain` too (5b78355 2026-06-08, "Grade terrain to
  the road").
- The **centre row** never existed in airoso: it arrived with v021 (861902c 2026-08-19, the k10-sd-v021 salvage).
- airoso took k10-colorado's rc14 hardening on 2026-07-23 (41d3d7e, #14: +388 lines in ribbon, +1,824 in build_mesh) —
  `_cap_offset`, the draped shoulder, banking — and before that Sand Creek's v0.6.4 `round_sharp_corners` (6f1447a
  2026-06-23). The flush-edge constants (`ROAD_LIFT_M` 0.0001, `GRASS_CLEARANCE_M` 0.01) are airoso's later "zero
  visible ledge" rule. v021 kept the June profile: road +0.10, grass −0.08 / −0.10, curb 0.14.
- v021 then grew only Lake Murray's own work (17 commits: tiered terrain 0046, ledger 0054's pieces / lamps / buildings,
  0057's σ windows). engine-july is k10-track-agents' prep at the July pin plus 0046 / 0061 (snapshot README).

## 3. The port plan

### What the assembler reads, and where a track's inputs come from

### The per-row column change (`tools/densify_ribbon_blend.py`)

The v021 ribbon is 3 vertices a row (left / centre / right), so a half-width between two rows is one quad of two
triangles. Where the bank or the width changes between rows the quad twists into a crease along its diagonal of
|(a + c) − (b + d)| / 2; engine-july pins the ground to the deck − 0.02 m vertex by vertex, and grass bridging a valley
crease stands over the lane. airoso's build_mesh answered the same twist with `ribbon_cols` (Exotics T2).

The contained version: a step AFTER everything that reads the 3-vertices-a-row layout (the weld and its
`mouth_weld.mesh_layout`, the seat), so no tool's contract changes. Every mainline half-quad whose crease exceeds 5 mm is
re-cut into k = ceil(crease / 5 mm) columns (up to 8): new vertices only ON the two row lines (each row is straight across
in plan and height, so the points are exact, nothing invented), the strip re-triangulated; a row edge shared by two quads
takes the larger k, so the mesh stays watertight. Connectors are left alone; no crease over 5 mm = not touched. Sand Creek:
83 half-quads (worst crease 16.5 mm, up to 4 columns); Nevada: 322 (18.3 mm, up to 4); High Plains: 104 (9.1 mm, 2). The
weld also gained two contained changes: it reads the connector edge from `route.edge` (same literals by default) and
accepts a track with no YLINE (a circuit paints no centre line).
