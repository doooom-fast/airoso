K10 - San Diego (V3) — Lake Murray Loop
Assetto Corsa track · github.com/k10-motorsports/k10-san-diego · doooom.fast

INSTALL
  Drag the zip into Content Manager, or unzip it so you have
    assettocorsa/content/tracks/san_diego_lake_murray/
  It replaces any earlier san_diego_lake_murray install. The v0.22.0 zip bundled a freeway-loop
  layout; that is gone — the San Diego Freeway Network is its own download beside this one.

NEEDS
  Custom Shaders Patch 0.2.x. The vegetation (CSP TreeFX, ~15,000 plants) and the ~240 street
  lights are CSP features; without it you get the bare road and no night lighting.
  Pure or Sol recommended.

WHAT IT IS
  A 13.5 km loop around Lake Murray reservoir: Alvarado Rd, College Ave, Navajo Rd and
  Lake Murray Blvd, with the Del Cerro Blvd side loop and the neighbourhood side streets off it.
  OpenStreetMap geometry on USGS 1 m elevation, then hand-finished: raised median islands with
  curbs, barriers and planting on College and Navajo; Del Cerro as a divided boulevard; side
  streets welded into the mainline with sidewalks that stop at every mouth; terrain seated under
  every road; 11.5 m street lamps. Start/finish, grid and pits sit on Alvarado Rd (the pits single
  file behind the grid). Each version ships its own AI line (<version>/ai/fast_lane.ai) for traffic and
  AI drive — a line computed from the road in that version's own lane, NOT recorded in game, so AI
  behaviour is unverified.

VERSIONS (pick one in Content Manager)
  Street Loop          — the open two-way streets as they are, start/finish on Alvarado Rd.
  Race — Clockwise     — one-way race track: Alvarado westbound, College northbound. Concrete
                         K-rail between the carriageways of the wide roads (never on Alvarado,
                         never on a median island), barriers along the kerbs, race kerbs at the
                         corners. Grid and pits on Alvarado in the north lane.
  Race — Anticlockwise — the same track the other way round, grid in the south lane.

KNOWN
  - Side-street terrain still floats a little (under 0.6 m) in a couple of Del Cerro spots.
  - The College Ave south hairpin and the lap seam are rougher than the rest of the loop.
