K10 - San Diego (V3) — Lake Murray Loop — credits
==================================================

Built by Kevin Conboy with the airoso track engine (k10-motorsports), hand-finished in Blender.
Everything bundled here is CC0-1.0 / public domain. Surface scrub and kerb-rumble sounds are the
player's own stock Assetto Corsa sounds (content/sfx), referenced by name in data/surfaces.ini —
none are redistributed here.

Data
  - OpenStreetMap road geometry (ODbL-1.0)        https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  - USGS 3DEP 1 m elevation (US public domain)    https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program

Our own work (CC0-1.0)
  - Road, kerb, sidewalk, median, terrain, lamp-post and W1-6 arrow-sign geometry (plate, arrow,
    post); AI line; map; outline
  - Lane lines, kerb stripe, the solid lamp and sign textures (generated:
    solid_sign_yellow.png, solid_sign_black.png)
  - MUTCD sign artwork mutcd_w1-6l/r, w1-7, w1-8l/r (generated; MUTCD sign designs are
    public-domain US Government works, 17 U.S.C. 105)
  - Street-lamp model: Kevin Conboy's own asset library

CC0 textures bundled in the kn5
  - aerial_asphalt_01 (road)          Poly Haven  CC0-1.0   https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_asphalt_01
  - Grass001 (grass)                  ambientCG   CC0-1.0   https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass001
  - Plaster004 (houses)               ambientCG   CC0-1.0   https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster004
  - Concrete034 (buildings)           ambientCG   CC0-1.0   https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034

Vegetation
  This public package ships NO tree models. The track was authored with CSP TreeFX plants
  (extension/trees/trees.txt lists all ~15,000 placements) using models from other mods that
  their authors have not licensed for redistribution. See extension/trees/README.txt to use
  your own TreeFX models.
