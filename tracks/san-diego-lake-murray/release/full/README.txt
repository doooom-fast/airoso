K10 - San Diego (V3) — Lake Murray Loop
Assetto Corsa track · github.com/k10-motorsports/k10-san-diego · doooom.fast

INSTALL
  Drag the zip into Content Manager, or unzip it so you have
    assettocorsa/content/tracks/san_diego_lake_murray/
  It replaces any earlier san_diego_lake_murray install. The v0.22.0 zip bundled a freeway-loop
  layout; that is gone — the San Diego Freeway Network is its own download beside this one.

NEEDS
  Custom Shaders Patch 0.2.x. The vegetation (CSP TreeFX, ~20,500 plants) and the ~320 street
  lights are CSP features; without it you get the bare road and no night lighting.
  Pure or Sol recommended.

WHAT IT IS
  A 13.5 km loop around Lake Murray reservoir: Alvarado Rd, College Ave, Navajo Rd and
  Lake Murray Blvd, with the Del Cerro Blvd side loop and the neighbourhood side streets off it.
  OpenStreetMap geometry on USGS 1 m elevation, then hand-finished: raised median islands with
  curbs, barriers and planting on College and Navajo; Del Cerro as a divided boulevard; side
  streets welded into the mainline with sidewalks that stop at every mouth; terrain seated under
  every road; 11.5 m street lamps. Start/finish, grid and pits sit on Alvarado Rd (the pits single
  file behind the grid). Each version ships its own AI line (<version>/ai/fast_lane.ai) for traffic and
  AI drive — a line computed from the road in that version's own lane, NOT recorded in game, so AI
  behaviour is unverified.

  Since v0.19.0 Free Roam also carries the north network: 6.4 km of two-way road joined to the loop
  at both ends - Waring Rd from College/Navajo to Princess View Dr, Princess View down into
  Grantville, Mission Gorge Rd east through the gorge under Cowles Mountain, and Jackson Dr north up
  the canyon back to Navajo - about 19.9 km of road in all. The 24 side streets off those roads are
  closed by concrete K-rail; the three new corners (Waring/Princess View, Princess View/Mission
  Gorge, Mission Gorge/Jackson) are real crossroads with curb returns. v0.19.1 dresses it: street
  lamps (Jackson's open canyon stays dark, as the real one is), large-arrow signs at the tight
  corners, the real rock cut walls on Mission Gorge and Jackson, trees placed from the aerial, and
  the buildings OpenStreetMap maps along them. The Speedways stay on the 13.5 km loop.

VERSIONS (pick one in Content Manager)
  Lake Murray Free Roam        — Lake Murray as it is: the open two-way streets, raised landscaped medians
                               on College and Navajo, the side streets open, no race furniture, plus
                               the 6.4 km north network (Waring, Princess View, Mission Gorge, Jackson
                               north) joined to the loop at both ends - about 19.9 km of road. Lap
                               clockwise from the line on Alvarado Rd.
  Lake Murray Speedway         — the same 13.5 km as a one-way race track: Alvarado westbound, College
                               northbound, Navajo eastbound, Jackson south, Lake Murray Blvd southwest.
                               You race an Alvarado-wide lane (about 11.4 m) on your own side of every
                               road: concrete K-rail along your curb and a second line closing off the
                               opposite lanes, running along the medians where there is one. The whole
                               street is yours on Alvarado and on the I-8 bridge deck; the full
                               intersection is open at the three corners the race turns at
                               (College/Navajo, Navajo/Jackson, Jackson/Lake Murray Blvd); side streets
                               are sealed; red/white kerbs at the corners. Grid and pits on Alvarado.
  Lake Murray Speedway Reverse — the same track the other way round: Alvarado eastbound, Lake Murray Blvd
                               northeast, Jackson north, Navajo westbound, College southbound, on your
                               own side again (its closing line stands in the other lanes).

KNOWN
  - Side-street terrain still floats a little (under 0.6 m) in a couple of Del Cerro spots.
  - The College Ave south hairpin and the lap seam are rougher than the rest of the loop.
  - The race versions have no inside kerb at College/Navajo or Navajo/Jackson: the road surface
    folds over itself on those two inside corners, and a kerb there would sit under the asphalt.
